## Anchor 1.0.0

A new look for the whole app, a Home page worth opening, and tasks that keep going while you look around.

### What's new

- **Home: release calendar and free games.** Upcoming game releases for the next 45 days, filterable by PC, PlayStation, Xbox and Switch. Hover a card to open it and bring its cover art into the background. Below it, games that are free to keep right now on Steam, Epic and GOG. Release data from RAWG (rawg.io), refreshed once a day.
- **New sidebar.** Grouped, animated and collapsible, with a dot next to any section that has a task running.
- **New look and backgrounds.** Frosted-glass cards with cut corners, glowing buttons and spring motion on every page, so every tab matches Home. Each of the 8 themes has its own background picture, or use one of yours from Account → Appearance.
- **Tasks keep running, with pop-ups.** Start a Health Check, cleanup, repair or scan and switch pages: it keeps going and keeps its result. When it finishes while you're somewhere else, a pop-up tells you, plus a Windows notification if Anchor isn't in front.
- **A new logo.** A redrawn anchor and a new signature orange, on the app icon, the taskbar, the tray and the sidebar.
- **Seasonal logos.** For Halloween (24 Oct – 1 Nov) the logo puts on a jack-o'-lantern, in the app and on the taskbar. Don't want it? Turn off **Seasonal logos** in Account → Appearance.
- **Drive temperature and disk rings.** Drive health always shows each drive's temperature, and every disk gets a used / free / total ring.

### Changes

- **Boost no longer frees memory.** One-click Boost on Home now clears the Cleanup page's default locations (temporary files, Windows Update and Delivery Optimization downloads, crash dumps, error reports and the system internet cache) and no longer trims background apps' RAM. Memory trim is now a separate, optional tool on the Cleanup page.
- **Restore-point settings moved from Home to Account.** How often Anchor makes a restore point before a change, and the "Create restore point" button, are now under Account.
- **A shorter sidebar.** Related tools are now tabs of one entry: Performance (Game & App Boost, Live monitor), Startup (Startup apps, Boot Watch), Debloat (Bloatware, Privacy) and System (Drive health, Diagnostics, Tools). "Boost & Cleanup" is now **Cleanup**.
- **Windows Update has its own sidebar item**, instead of being a tab elsewhere.
- **Animations no longer follow Windows' "Animation effects" setting.** Anchor animates by default. If you'd rather have less motion, turn on **Reduce animations** in Account → Appearance.
