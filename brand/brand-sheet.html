Brand v2

A redrawn anchor, a new signature orange and a lowercase wordmark, plus seasonal versions of the icon. Every file here is generated from one geometry, so the marks, icons and lockups always match.

The mark

Three shapes in one weight. The ring floats free of the shank: it's the point you can always return to, the way every change Anchor makes starts with a restore point.

Ring
Open circle, 9 units thick on a 100-unit grid. The counter stays at least as wide as the stroke, so it reads as a ring, never a dot.
Gap
6 units between ring and shank. It closes to 5 in the small version.
Shank
11 units, round-capped on top, merged into the bowl.
Bowl
A constant-weight arc, 11 units, ends cut flat just above the horizontal.
Small
At 32 px and under, use the heavier small mark (14-unit weight).

App icon

White mark on a Buoy-orange tile. Ink and Foam tiles are for places where the orange would fight its surroundings.

Colour

Buoy is lifebuoy orange, the colour of the thing that keeps you afloat. It sets Anchor apart from the wall of blue system tools. Click a value to copy it.

Harbour

Foam

Light ground. Never pure white behind the mark.

Seasonal

Each season swaps only the ring and dresses the tile, so the anchor stays recognisable. The app switches automatically by date, and Account → Appearance → Seasonal logos turns it off. Halloween is live now; the rest are finished and saved in the app, switched off until you turn them on.

Using it

Do

  • Keep clear space around the mark equal to the ring’s outer diameter.
  • Use the small mark at 32 px and under; it’s drawn heavier on purpose.
  • Put the orange mark on Foam or Harbour. On orange, use the white mark.
  • Use the one-colour lockup when you only have one ink.

Don’t

  • Close the gap between the ring and the shank.
  • Recolour the mark outside the palette, or put the orange mark on photos.
  • Set “anchor” in another typeface, or capitalise the wordmark.
  • Mix seasonal marks with the standard icon on the same screen.
